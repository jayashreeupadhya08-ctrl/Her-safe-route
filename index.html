What is the Silent Signal?

The Signal for Help is a single-handed gesture that can be shown discretely over video calls, through a window, or to a bystander on the street without leaving any digital trace or escalating immediate danger.

How To Execute It:

  1. Hold up hand: Palm facing outward toward the person you are alerting.
  2. Tuck thumb: Fold your thumb inward across your palm.
  3. Trap fingers: Close your other four fingers down over your thumb to trap it.

The 5 D's of Active Bystander Intervention:

  • Distract: De-escalate by asking a casual question ("Do you know when the next bus comes?").
  • Delegate: Alert an authority figure, shopkeeper, or transit marshal.
  • Document: Keep a safe distance, take notes or verify location for emergency services.
  • Delay: Check in on the person after the situation has diffused.
  • Direct: Intervene only if it is completely safe to do so.
